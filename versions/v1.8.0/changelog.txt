### Changes

*A comprehensive list of mod changes can be found [here](<https://github.com/McSnurtle/Project-Skyfall/compare/v1.7.1...v1.8>)
**Gameplay Changes**
 - Removed crafting recipe for `alekiships:cannon` (credit: @Nathaniel2011 )
 - Updated server motd (see **Update Preview :eyes:**)
- ~~Added medieval siege weapons for early-game warfare (`machines`) ([Repo](<>), [Modrinth](<>)) (#307 )~~
> - ~~Added ballista, a manned turret that packs a punch!~~
> - ~~Added battering ram, perfect for slowly smashing through castle walls~~
> - ~~Added siege cannon, a more heavy duty version of the Nifty Ships cannon, yet easier than the late game, *super heavy duty cannons*~~
> - ~~Added catapult, a long-range, super-slow, beast of a wall destroyer~~
> - ~~Added siege ladder, a difficult and risky tool for scaling large walls... Perhaps it would be best to have them on a cliff or on the water?~~
*(The siege machines are unfortunately not updated to a compatible version, and might be added in the future if the mod isn't abandoned. Manually decompiling and updating would be **a lot** of work.)*
 - Added Minecraft: Legends-style command-able armies (`recruits`) ([Repo](<>), [Modrinth](<>)) (#309 )
> - Villagers can now be recruited into a player's army - for the right price
> - Villager recruits have different stats, very similarly to colonists, and also very similarly, they get upgraded as they get more kills
> - Villager recruits come in classes: bowman, recruit shieldman, recruit, nomad, horseman, and crossbowman
> - Recruited villagers can be assigned groups, like any good RTS, and instructed based on class, or group
> - Highly skilled recruits can be found in rare caravans around the world - with high skill comes a high price. New recruits can be acquired and trained through the recruit job blocks; soon to be craftable, and currently purchasable from apprentice level weapon-smiths and armorers
> - Recruitment tables are unable to be claimed, meaning placing one down guarantees infinite recruits of that type
> - There is an absurd amount of depth to this mod!! A crazy amount!! Players can have a group assigned as personal protection, with a band of archers set to stand their ground and shoot a tower of enemy reinforcements - behind the row of shieldman, who are advancing *with* the swordsman
 - Added immunity frames on roll. Players will no longer take damage just before, during, and a while after rolling. Multi-roll for the win!
 - Reduced campfire's healing radius from 16 to 8 blocks (#308 )
 - Substantially optimized performance around create mod factories, and general particle optimizations (`mods/particle_core-0.2.5+1.20.1+forge.jar`) ([Repo](<>), [Modrinth](<https://modrinth.com/mod/particle-core/version/0.2.5+1.20.1+forge>))
 - Added new dependency mod (`mods/fzzy_config-0.6.3+1.20.1+forge.jar`) ([Repo](<>), [Modrinth](<https://modrinth.com/mod/fzzy-config/version/0.6.2+1.20.1+forge>))
